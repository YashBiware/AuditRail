4. Solution/Action taken:

     SHA-256 sequential chaining) anchored periodically to immutable cloud storage
     (AWS S3 Object Lock / WORM compliant). This guarantees sub-millisecond
     latency, zero gas fees, and verifiable mathematical immutability.
